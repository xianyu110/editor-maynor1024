隐私政策

最近更新日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日

本政策适用于 MaynorAI 文章同步助手，包括产品网站 product.maynor1024.live、在线编辑器 editor.maynor1024.live，以及名为「MaynorAI文章同步助手」的浏览器扩展。我们重视你的数据，并在此说明我们收集什么、如何使用，以及不会做什么。

1. 我们收集和使用的信息

账户与编辑器内容

当你在编辑器注册或登录时，我们会处理你的账户信息，例如电子邮箱，以及你选择使用的 Google 或 GitHub 登录信息（由相应平台向我们提供的账户标识和基本资料）。你保存在云端文章夹中的标题、正文、图片和其他编辑内容，会存储在我们的服务器及存储服务中，用于向你提供写作、保存、同步和历史记录功能。

未登录时，草稿可能只保存在你正在使用的这台浏览器中，不会自动上传到云端。

浏览器扩展与平台登录信息

扩展用于在你已登录的平台页面上创建或更新草稿。各平台的登录态、Cookie、账号名称，以及你主动添加的 GitHub Token 等凭据，默认只保存在这台电脑的扩展存储中，不会上传到 MaynorAI 的服务器。你不必把各平台的账号密码交给我们。

当你在编辑器中发起同步时，扩展会在你的电脑上把你选定的文章内容发送到你勾选的目标平台。默认同步为草稿；只有在你打开「同步后直接发布」时，扩展才会尝试发布（X 长文不会自动点击发布）。

如果你启用可选的本机连接（例如在扩展中保存本地服务 Token），该 Token 同样留在你的电脑上，仅用于连接你配置的本机服务。

付费与人工智能功能

若你购买套餐或积分，支付由 Stripe 处理。我们会保存与订单、套餐和积分余额相关的记录，但不会存储完整的银行卡号。

若你使用「AI 润色」「文章插图」或「做封面」等功能，为了完成你的请求，你提交的文本或图片可能会被发送给我们的模型服务提供商。我们不会把这些内容用于出售，也不会用它来公开训练面向他人的产品资料。

网站访问数据

编辑器网站使用我们自行部署的访问统计服务，用于了解页面是否可正常打开、访问次数和基本访问环境（例如页面地址、来源、浏览器类型和大致地区）。该统计不会收集你的文章正文，也不会收集各平台登录凭据。

2. 我们如何共享信息

我们不会出售你的个人信息。仅在以下情况下，信息才会被提供给第三方：

  • 你主动要求同步或发布时，将文章内容发送到你勾选的平台；
  • 你使用 AI 功能时，向模型服务提供商发送完成请求所需的内容；
  • 登录或支付时，向 Google、GitHub 或 Stripe 等服务提供必要信息；
  • 为运营网站和存储内容而使用的基础设施提供商；
  • 法律要求，或为保护用户与服务安全所必需的情形。

3. 保存期限与安全

账户和云端文章会在你使用服务期间保存；你删除文章或注销账户后，我们会在合理期限内删除或匿名化相关数据，除非法律要求继续保留。保存在你电脑上的扩展凭据，由你通过卸载扩展、清除扩展数据或移出已保存账号来管理。

我们采取合理的技术与管理措施保护数据，但无法保证互联网传输或存储绝对安全。

4. 你的选择

你可以查看、编辑或删除云端文章，更新账户资料，卸载扩展，或停止使用付费与 AI 功能。如需查阅、更正、导出或删除与账户相关的个人信息，请发送邮件至 bgnwd15@gmail.com，我们会在合理期限内处理。

5. 儿童

本服务面向成年人。我们不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果发现此类数据，请与我们联系，我们会尽快删除。

6. 政策更新

我们可能会更新本政策。更新后会在本页面公布新的日期和内容。若变更实质影响你的权利，我们会通过网站或账户相关方式提示。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策或个人信息处理有疑问，请发送邮件至 bgnwd15@gmail.com，或通过 编辑器 登录后与我们联系。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy applies to MaynorAI Article Sync, including the product site at product.maynor1024.live, the editor at editor.maynor1024.live, and the browser extension named “MaynorAI文章同步助手”.

1. Information we collect and use

Account and editor content. When you register or sign in, we process account information such as your email address and, if you choose Google or GitHub sign-in, the account identifiers and basic profile data those services provide to us. Titles, body text, images, and other content you save to the cloud library are stored on our servers and storage providers so we can provide writing, saving, syncing, and history features. If you are not signed in, drafts may stay only in the browser you are using and are not automatically uploaded.

Extension and platform credentials. The extension creates or updates drafts on platforms where you are already signed in. Platform sessions, cookies, account names, and credentials you add yourself (such as a GitHub token) are stored in the extension on your computer by default and are not uploaded to MaynorAI servers. You do not need to give us your platform passwords. When you start a sync in the editor, the extension sends the selected article from your computer to the platforms you choose. Syncing defaults to drafts; the extension attempts to publish only if you turn on “publish after sync” (it will not auto-click publish for X Articles). If you enable the optional local connection (for example a local service token in the extension), that token also stays on your computer and is used only to reach the local service you configured.

Payments and AI features. If you buy a plan or credits, payments are processed by Stripe. We keep records related to orders, plans, and credit balances, but we do not store full payment card numbers. If you use features such as AI polish, article illustrations, or cover generation, the text or images you submit may be sent to our model service providers to complete your request. We do not sell this content and do not use it to build public training materials for others.

Site analytics. The editor site uses our self-hosted analytics to understand whether pages load, visit counts, and basic visit context (such as page URL, referrer, browser type, and approximate region). This analytics service does not collect article body text or platform login credentials.

2. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only when needed to operate the service, including: sending article content to the platforms you select when you request a sync or publish; sending content required to fulfill an AI request to model providers; providing necessary information to Google, GitHub, or Stripe for sign-in or payment; using infrastructure providers to host the site and store content; and when required by law or to protect users and the service.

3. Retention and security

Account data and cloud articles are kept while you use the service. After you delete an article or close your account, we delete or anonymize related data within a reasonable period, unless we must keep it by law. Credentials stored in the extension on your computer are managed by you, for example by removing saved accounts, clearing extension data, or uninstalling the extension. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Your choices

You may view, edit, or delete cloud articles, update your account, uninstall the extension, or stop using paid and AI features. To access, correct, export, or delete personal information associated with your account, email bgnwd15@gmail.com, and we will handle the request within a reasonable time.

5. Children

This service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have done so, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new date and content on this page. If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide a notice on the site or through your account.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or our handling of personal information, email bgnwd15@gmail.com, or reach us after signing in at the editor.
